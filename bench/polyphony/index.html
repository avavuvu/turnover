Polyphony benchmark

Many voices play at the same time. Each voice uses one clip and jumps to a random position every 125 ms, together with all other voices. All voices draw into one WebGL2 canvas.

  • On time: part of (voice × animation frame) where the planned frame is on screen.
  • Frames / s: frames delivered / frames needed, for all voices together.
  • Latency: time from frame request until the frame is in its texture.
  • Longest tick gap: longest time between two animation frames. More than ~17 ms is a dropped display frame.
